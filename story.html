Story & Lore

Uncover the dark history of the Dreadwood Forest

Prologue: The Lost Expedition

You awaken in a moonlit clearing, head pounding, memories fragmented. The last thing you remember is joining the Harrington Expedition—a team of researchers sent to investigate the legends of Dreadwood Forest.

Now you are alone. Your camp is gone. Your companions are gone. The forest stretches endlessly in every direction, and something out there is watching.

Through the fog and twisted trees, you glimpse shapes that should not exist. Old research notes flutter in the wind, speaking of "the Breathing Dark" and "the First Hunger."

Your only way out is through the heart of the forest—to find what remains of your team, and uncover the truth behind the forest's hunger.

Story Walkthrough

Act I: Awakening

  • 1. Search the initial clearing for supplies and the first journal entry
  • 2. Follow the broken branches east to find the abandoned camp
  • 3. Retrieve the expedition map from the supply tent
  • 4. Encounter your first Hollow—choose whether to spare or fight
  • 5. Reach the Old Watchtower before nightfall

Key Decision: The first Hollow you meet can guide you or curse you. Spare it to gain a permanent directional hint. Attack it for early bone charms.

Act II: The Deep Wood

  • 1. Navigate the maze-like undergrowth using landmark clues
  • 2. Discover the Creekside Shrine and offer a bone charm
  • 3. Evade The Watcher in the Old Growth district
  • 4. Find Dr. Marsh's second journal at the Ranger Station
  • 5. Learn about the "Feeding Grounds" ceremony

Key Decision: The Creekside Shrine accepts different offerings. White bone charms reveal safe paths. Red bone charms reveal creature weaknesses.

Act III: The Heart of Dread

  • 1. Locate the three shrine fragments hidden throughout the forest
  • 2. Choose your path: The Path of Light or The Path of Shadow
  • 3. Confront the entity known as "The First Hunger"
  • 4. Make your final choice that determines the ending

Key Decision: This choice affects the entire ending. No decision is purely good or evil—the forest operates on different morality.

Lore: The First Hunger

Long before human settlers arrived, the forest was home to the Hollow People—spirits who maintained the balance between growth and decay. They tended the woods for millennia, ensuring no single species dominated.

Then came the Arrival—the meteor that struck the heart of the forest. It brought with it the First Hunger, an entity of pure consumption that fed not on flesh but on existence itself. It devoured the Hollow People one by one, corrupting their forms into the twisted creatures that roam today.

The corruption spreads slowly. Each year, more of the forest falls silent. The remaining Hollow are memories of what they once were, forever mourning what was lost.

The meteor's crater still exists at the forest's center. It pulses with a faint red light, and those who approach report hearing whispers in languages that predate human speech.

Key Characters

Dr. Elena Marsh

Team leader and xenoanthropologist. Her journals reveal she knew more about the forest's danger than she let on. Fate: Unknown.

Marcus Webb

The expedition's guide. Experienced woodsman who claimed to know every trail. He vanished on the first night. Fate: Captured by Crawlers.

The Watcher

Not truly evil—merely doing what it was created to do. Some say it was the first Hollow, corrupted beyond recognition. Others claim it is the forest itself, given form.

The Voice in the Dark

A mysterious presence that speaks directly to the player. It offers knowledge at a price. Its true nature is unknown, but it predates even the First Hunger.

Endings Guide

Ending A: The Breaking Dawn

Destroy the First Hunger completely. This also destroys the forest and all corrupted creatures. You escape, but the land remains dead. "Some prices are worth paying."

Ending B: The New Balance

Restore the Hollow People using the combined shrine power. The forest heals slowly, but you cannot leave. You become the new caretaker. "The forest remembers."

Ending C: The Eternal Hunt

Embrace the First Hunger's power. You become the new entity, hunting through forests across the world. "Hunger is the only truth."

Ending D: The Forgotten

The hidden ending. Achieve perfect silence, no creature awareness, all Hollow spared. Become one with the forest as a guardian spirit. "You were never here at all."
