Survival Tips

Essential strategies to survive the Dreadwood

🔇 Sound Management

Sound is your greatest enemy in the Dreadwood. Every footstep, every breath, every rustle of clothing can attract deadly attention.

Key Techniques

  • Sneak Walking (Hold Shift): Reduces footstep volume by 80% but slows movement significantly
  • Hold Breath (Hold Space): Prevents breathing sounds that The Watcher can detect within 30 meters
  • Crouching: Further reduces sound footprint and makes you harder to spot visually
  • Environmental Cover: Dense foliage, fog areas, and near water features mask sound

Pro Tip: Rain is your friend. Heavy rain completely masks footsteps but reduces visibility.

💡 Light Management

Finding the balance between visibility and detection is crucial. Light attracts creatures, but complete darkness increases other dangers.

Flashlight (F key)

  • • Reveals hidden items and paths
  • • Can temporarily stun Crawlers
  • • Attracts The Watcher if used carelessly
  • • Use in short bursts to conserve battery

Glow Items

  • • Mushroom clusters provide safe ambient light
  • • Bone charms glow faintly (safe to use)
  • • Red mushrooms attract predators
  • • White mushrooms repel some creatures

👁️ Creature Avoidance Strategies

Avoiding The Watcher

  • • Listen for clicking sounds—means it's hunting nearby
  • • Stop moving entirely when you hear it approach
  • • Never run unless directly threatened
  • • The Watcher cannot detect low-frequency movements
  • • Red mushroom areas attract it—avoid or use as distraction

Avoiding The Crawlers

  • • They follow sounds persistently—create a distraction
  • • Running draws more of the pack
  • • Kill the alpha first to disorient the group
  • • They avoid direct fire—torches are essential
  • • They cannot climb—elevated positions are safe

Avoiding The Shadow Stalker

  • • Never look directly at mirrors or reflective surfaces
  • • Keep a light source active at all times
  • • It appears in your peripheral vision first—turn away
  • • Direct sunlight forces it to retreat
  • • White bone charms provide sanity protection

🎒 Resource Management

Supplies are scarce. Every item counts, and knowing what to prioritize can mean the difference between survival and a grisly end.

Priority Items

Bone Charms Always keep 2-3 in reserve
Salt Essential for The Breathing Tree
Torch/Fire Source Repels most creatures
Journal Pages Unlock shortcuts and hints
Red Mushrooms Emergency distraction

🧠 Sanity Management

Your sanity degrades when witnessing horrors, spending too long in darkness, or encountering the Shadow Stalker. Low sanity causes visual distortions and slower reactions.

Sanity Restoration

  • • White bone charms restore 25% sanity
  • • Staying in moderate light stabilizes sanity decay
  • • Finding journal pages provides mental relief
  • • Safe rests at shrines (when protected) restore sanity
  • • Avoid looking at the Shadow Stalker directly

Warning Signs of Low Sanity

Screen edge darkening, hearing whispers, seeing creatures that aren't there, movement controls becoming unresponsive. When sanity reaches 0%, you become vulnerable to possession.
