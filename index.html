Getting Started

Quick Tips

  • 1. Stay near light sources but not too close - creatures are drawn to both light and sound
  • 2. Hold your breath when you hear clicking sounds - The Watcher is nearby
  • 3. Never run unless absolutely necessary - running creates vibrations that travel far
  • 4. Collect bone charms early - they provide crucial protection against sanity loss
